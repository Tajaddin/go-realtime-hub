# go-realtime-hub fan-out benchmark
date:      2026-11-06
hardware:  Intel i5-13600KF 14c/20t; 32 GB RAM; Windows 11
runtime:   Go 1.27.3 (in-process pub/sub; no network)
command:   go run ./load -subscribers 10000 -messages 200

Throughput depends heavily on machine load because the publisher and
10,000 consumer goroutines compete for CPU. These runs were taken on a
machine shared with other jobs (total CPU 68-90% when sampled before
and after the runs), so they spread widely and are a loaded-machine
figure, not an idle one; the median is the number to quote.
